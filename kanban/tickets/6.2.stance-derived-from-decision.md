# 6.2 One stance line, its verb derived from the decision

## Outcome
Option A opens with one stance line: the verb comes from `decision`, the sentence comes from the
seat, and no verdict field can make the two disagree.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given any verdict, Then option A opens with one stance line whose verb is derived from `decision` and whose sentence is the seat's own (plan 6 AC-7).
- AC-2 (property): For all verdicts, including one whose seat wrote a stance contradicting its own decision, the stance verb printed equals the one `decision` implies — a test asserts no field combination makes them disagree (plan 6 AC-7).
- AC-3 (behavioral): Given a verdict whose seat wrote no stance sentence, Then the stance line prints the derived verb alone and the block stays inside its 40-line cap.

## Out of scope
- Changing what `decision` means, or when a verdict is `reject`.
- The Gate 2 page hero and banner, which already read from `decision`.
- Any second authority on the ship question.
