# 5.2 A light ticket over the budget is stopped and sent to grill

## Outcome
After `feat-commit` the runner counts the light ticket's changed lines: at or under 150 it carries on as today, over it stops the run, drops the branch and supersedes the ticket with the grill command in its Log.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given a ticket carrying `lane: light`, When the runner's `feat-commit` phase is done, Then the runner counts insertions plus deletions against the base branch over the ticket's `writes:` scope and the included paths, and at or under 150 continues to `ci` exactly as today (plan 5 AC-6)
- AC-2 (behavioral): Given that count is over 150, Then the runner writes `done error light over budget` naming the count and the budget, deletes the `ticket/<id>` branch, sets the ticket `superseded` with a Log entry naming `/gates:grill <n>`, calls no verdict seat, exits 2, and starts no grill of its own (plan 5 AC-7)
- AC-3 (behavioral): Given a ticket carrying `lane: full` or no `lane:` at all, When the same phase is done, Then no count is made and no phase is added: the run is byte-identical to today (plan 5 AC-6)
- AC-4 (behavioral): Given a light ticket that stays under the budget and reaches Gate 2, Then `traces/verdict/<id>.{input.md,json,summary.json,html}` are written as for any ticket and the result block is what the session prints, through the same five Gate 2 words and the same `kanban_ops.py` calls (plan 5 AC-8)

- The lane record and the docs — 5.3
- Starting the grill from the runner; it stops and names the command
- A light flag in `render_verdict.py`: the Gate 2 page is written as today
