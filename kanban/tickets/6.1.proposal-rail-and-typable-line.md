# 6.1 A seat-proposed action survives the rail and lands as a typable line

## Outcome
One open finding's seat-proposed `action` and `why` pass through the schema and the rail and
appear in option A as a Gate 2 line the human types as printed, the pre-filled `waive` included,
with a refused proposal falling back to the computed action and saying so.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given a verdict JSON whose open finding carries `action` and `why`, When `render_verdict.py` builds option A, Then that finding's line is the Gate 2 line for that action — `child from F#`, `home F# to <id>` or `waive F#: <the seat's why>` — and the `why` text appears in the line as the seat wrote it (plan 6 AC-1).
- AC-2 (critical): Given an open finding with `severity: block` and `action: waive`, When the rail validates the verdict, Then the proposal is refused, the action from `recommendations()` is used in its place, and the stored verdict JSON records the refusal with the finding id and the reason it was refused (plan 6 AC-2).
- AC-3 (behavioral): Given a proposal naming a verb outside the five Gate 2 verbs, or a `home` target that is not an open ticket, or a `child` on a ticket other than this one, Then the same refusal path runs and `recommendations()` supplies that finding's action (plan 6 AC-3).
- AC-4 (property): For all verdicts, every open finding in option A has exactly one typable line and every line option A prints is one of the five Gate 2 verbs — a test asserts it over the archived verdicts in `traces/verdict/` (plan 6 AC-4).
- AC-5 (behavioral): Given at least one refused proposal, Then the result block carries one line naming how many were refused and pointing at the page, and the page's "Recommended action" section names each refused finding and why; Given none were refused, Then the block carries no such line (plan 6 AC-5).
- AC-6 (behavioral): Given a verdict whose findings carry no `action` — every verdict written before this ticket — Then option A is built from `recommendations()` exactly as today and the block says nothing about refusals (plan 6 AC-6).
- AC-7 (critical): Given the rail refuses a proposal, Then it substitutes and reports and never exits non-zero on that account, and a test asserts the verdict is still rendered and the run still reaches Gate 2.

## Out of scope
- The stance line: 6.2.
- The duplicate group and the second-sighting flip: 6.3.
- Rewriting the prompt's own prose beyond the fields this ticket adds: 6.4.
- A sixth Gate 2 verb, a group waive, or any change to `kanban_ops.py`.
