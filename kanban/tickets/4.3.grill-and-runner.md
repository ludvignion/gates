# 4.3 The grill creates issues and the runner walks them

## Outcome
After Gate 1 in an opted-in project the grill creates one issue per slice through `kanban_ops.py new`, rewrites the plan's slices to `#<number>`, and the runner walks and polls them by label.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given an approved plan and the marker, When `kanban_ops.py new <plan> <slug> --body-file <path>` runs per slice, Then one issue per slice exists labelled `ticket` and `ready` with `parent: <n>` and `depends_on` in the body, and the grill rewrites `## Slices` to `#<number>` in one commit; `schemas.Plan.slices` parses `#<number>` (plan 4 AC-7)
- AC-2 (behavioral): Given the runner walks a plan and waits at Gate 2, When the status label becomes `done`, Then the walk continues in `depends_on` order computed from the mirror's `parent` and `depends_on` (plan 4 AC-9)
- AC-3 (behavioral): Given a ticket id that is an issue number, When `verdict_prep.py` and `verdict_checks.py` run, Then the packet's ticket text comes from the mirror and the plan is found by `parent`, not by splitting the id
- AC-4 (behavioral): Given the grill skill, Then rule 9 names `kanban_ops.py new` for opted-in projects and file tickets otherwise; a test pins it (plan 4 AC-0)
