# 6.5 Shipping records a disposition for every finding left open

## Outcome
A ship says what happened to the findings nobody routed, so an open finding is a recorded
decision rather than a silence the lint re-reports for the rest of the project's life.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given a ticket shipped with open findings that carry no verb line, Then `kanban_ops.py ship` writes one Log line `shipped open: <ids>` naming every one of them.
- AC-2 (behavioral): Given a finding named in a `shipped open:` line, Then `lint_kanban.finding_homes()` treats it as addressed and does not report it.
- AC-3 (critical): Given a ticket shipped with every finding routed, Then no `shipped open:` line is written, and a test asserts the empty case adds nothing to the Log.
- AC-4 (critical): Given the 6.1 Log after ship — 13 open warns and no disposition — as a fixture, Then a shipped-open line names all 13 and `lint_kanban` reports none of them.
- AC-5 (behavioral): Given a `shipped open:` line, Then it records that the ticket shipped with these findings open and unfixed, and no code path renders or counts it as a waiver.

## Out of scope
- Backfilling 6.1's already-shipped Log. It keeps its silence and the lint keeps flagging it
  until a human adds the line by hand.
- Any change to what the verdict emits, or to the five Gate 2 verbs.
- The nesting in `lint_kanban.py:148`, which is the findings-pipeline brief's.
