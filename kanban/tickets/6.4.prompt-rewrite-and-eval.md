# 6.4 The prompt rewritten, and scored against the one it replaces

## Outcome
`verdict-prompt.md` is rewritten to say what a complete recommendation is and what the rail
refuses, with no routing branches and nothing the tool-less one-turn seat cannot follow, and the
rewrite is scored against the previous prompt over this repo's archived packets.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 (behavioral): Given `skills/verdict/verdict-prompt.md` after the rewrite, Then it states what a complete recommendation contains, names what the rail refuses, enumerates no per-severity routing branch, and carries no instruction that needs a tool call or a second turn to follow (plan 6 AC-10).
- AC-2 (property): For all archived packets under `traces/verdict/`, `verdict_eval.py` scores the rewritten prompt and the previous one over the same packets and prints `decision_agreement` for both, and the run makes no network call beyond the verdict command itself (plan 6 AC-11).
- AC-3 (behavioral): Given `README.md` and `docs/architecture.md`, Then each says the Gate 2 recommendation is proposed by the seat and validated by code, and names the one non-negotiable: a block is never waived by a proposal (plan 6 AC-12).
- AC-4 (critical): Given the rewritten prompt, Then a test asserts every field the output shape names is one the schema knows and every field the schema requires is one the prompt names, so the prompt and `scripts/schemas.py` cannot drift.
- AC-5 (human): Given one real ticket at Gate 2 after this ticket ships, a person confirms option A was typable exactly as printed and told them something the severity list did not (plan 6 AC-13).

## Out of scope
- Moving any part of the prompt to a sibling file. The seat runs with no tools and the packet is
  its whole world, so the prompt shortens by deleting, not by pointing (plan 6, technical
  decision 5).
- Seeding `traces/golden/` or giving `replay.py` a grill `--replay` mode.
- Changing how findings are judged: severity, impact, consequence and the citation rule keep
  their present meaning.
- Adding a recommendation-quality metric to `verdict_eval.py`; this ticket compares the metrics
  that already exist.
